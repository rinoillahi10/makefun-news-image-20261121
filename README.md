A one-image front page summing up the world news of Saturday
21 November 2026, for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261121`.

## The image

`entry.png` is a 1024x1024 newspaper front page (JPEG bytes):

- **Masthead** — "THE WORLD TODAY" with the date
  "SATURDAY 21 NOVEMBER 2026" in large bold type.
- **Three headline stories**, each with an original editorial
  illustration and a short factual caption:
  1. **World Court: Myanmar guilty of Rohingya genocide** —
     landmark ruling at The Hague finds state-sponsored genocide.
  2. **Russian missiles fall in Poland** — Warsaw calls it a
     deliberate provocation; NATO calls it a grave violation of
     its airspace.
  3. **Stocks plunge as AI shares slide** — S&P 500 and Dow enter
     correction, down 3.7 percent.
- **Footer strip** — Trump, Xi and Putin leave the first
  trilateral summit with few results; COP31 climate talks enter
  their final stage in Turkey; Cyclone Akvan hits India's east
  coast; UK and EU sign food, carbon and youth mobility deals;
  Saudi coalition intercepts Houthi missiles over Riyadh.

Every headline was verified against the day's reporting before it
was typeset. The scene pictures are original AI-generated
editorial-style illustrations; all text in the image is legible,
with the date prominent so the day it sums up is unmistakable.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`),
byte-identical on every run.
